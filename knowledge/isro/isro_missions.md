# Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) Earth Observation Missions

## Overview of ISRO Remote Sensing Program
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) operates one of the largest civilian constellations of Earth Observation (EO) satellites in the world, deployed under the Indian Remote Sensing (IRS) program for agricultural monitoring, water resources management, forestry, mineral prospecting, oceanography, disaster management, and cartography.

## Key Satellite Constellations

### 1. RISAT Series (Radar Imaging Satellite) / EOS-04
- **RISAT-1 / EOS-04**: Active C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) operating at 5.35 GHz with multi-polarization (HH, HV, VH, VV) and circular polarization capability.
- **Applications**: Crucial for all-weather agricultural crop estimation (kharif season during heavy monsoon cloud cover), flood inundation mapping in the Brahmaputra and Ganga basins, and coastal sea-ice monitoring.

### 2. Cartosat Series
- High-resolution stereoscopic optical satellites designed for cartographic applications, urban cadastre, infrastructure planning, and Digital Elevation Model (DEM) generation.
- **Cartosat-2 & 3**: Sub-meter spatial resolution (panchromatic resolution down to 0.28 m in Cartosat-3, multispectral down to 1.1 m).

### 3. Resourcesat Series
- Multi-spectral payloads providing continuous multi-resolution monitoring:
  - **LISS-4**: High resolution (5.8 m spatial resolution, 3 bands).
  - **LISS-3**: Medium resolution (23.5 m spatial resolution, 4 bands).
  - **AWiFS** (Advanced Wide Field Sensor): Wide swath (740 km, 56 m spatial resolution, 5-day revisit), vital for national-scale agricultural drought assessments.

### 4. Oceansat Series
- Geared toward ocean color monitoring, chlorophyll concentration retrieval, suspended sediment dynamics, and sea surface winds (via Ku-band scatterometer).

### 5. NISAR (NASA-ISRO SAR)
- Flagship collaborative dual-frequency L-band (1.25 GHz, NASA) and S-band (3.2 GHz, ISRO) polarimetric radar mission.
- Designed to observe global environmental change, crustal deformation, glacier velocities, wetland hydrology, and forest biomass with sub-centimeter surface displacement precision.
